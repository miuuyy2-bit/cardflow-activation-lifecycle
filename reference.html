Editorial model The five states are a reasoning framework, not a universal issuer protocol.

Source boundary Every linked source supports only the claim described in the dataset.

Privacy boundary No card number, PIN, claim code, receipt or account credential is accepted.

Machine-readable specification

One record per checkpoint

Each state names its question, evidence boundary, responsible systems and safety note. Source IDs connect individual records to the official references that support them.

Identity

id + position

Stable keys preserve the order of the simplified lifecycle.

Meaning

checkpoint + definition

The exact question answered at this state.

Evidence boundary

canSupport + cannotProve

Positive evidence and explicit limits stay side by side.

Routing

primaryOwner + usefulEvidence

Names the system that can verify the state and the records worth keeping.

Reuse notes

What this resource can—and cannot—do

Good uses

  • Annotate an educational article or support workflow.
  • Validate a compatible lifecycle dataset.
  • Embed the SVG with accessible alternative text.
  • Compare evidence claims without testing a live code.

Outside the model

  • Determining whether an individual card is valid.
  • Checking balance, rate, ownership or acceptance.
  • Replacing current issuer terms or support.
  • Making a recovery, refund or transaction promise.

Source register

Claims stay attached to their limits

Apple Support, Google Play Help and the CFPB source were rechecked on 31 August 2026. Mastercard was last checked on 16 August 2026.
